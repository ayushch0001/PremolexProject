Who We Are

Established in 2008 in Raipur, Premolex Industries has grown to become a cornerstone in the manufacturing of robust fluid handling solutions. We specialize in engineering high-density polyethylene infrastructure designed to withstand extreme operational conditions.

Our commitment to rigorous quality control and technical innovation ensures that every product leaving our facility meets strict international standards for reliability and longevity.
